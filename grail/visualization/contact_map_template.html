Data and interpretation

Each hand has its own source-reviewed phase schedule and independent original human/object vertex fields. Unknown and no-contact intervals have zero active fields. Overlapping region strengths combine with max. Acquisition and hold can use different regions. No nearest-point replacement or one-to-one correspondence is created.

Colors interpolate strengths on original mesh triangles. Mesh seam copies are counted as native vertex IDs, with spatially distinct positions reported separately. No added surface samples or remeshed assets are used in this viewer. Density does not measure contact accuracy. Hidden finger identities and thumb opposition retain lower evidence strength.
